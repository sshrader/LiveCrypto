    Rundown

      How this rundown was made

      • A small server reads news feeds and DexScreener every few minutes, drops duplicates and old items, and has Claude write a short segment for each new one. Every segment links to its source and shows when it was published.
      • Numbers in a segment are checked against the source text before it can air, and the market stats are filled in by code rather than by the writer. Segments that fail the check are thrown away.
      • This page asks the server for new segments every 30 seconds and airs the unseen ones first. Studio voices appear when the server has an AI voice key; otherwise your browser voices read the script.

      Read this first

      Segments are written by an AI from news summaries and market data, and can still be wrong or out of date. Numbers are as reported at the time shown, not live prices. DexScreener boosts are paid promotion, not a recommendation. Always open the source. Nothing here is financial advice.
